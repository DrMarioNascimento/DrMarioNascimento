### 🇺🇸 Hi, I'm Prof. Mário César Nascimento, PhD

University professor of **Physiology** (Physical Education and Physiotherapy), **Ethics** and **research training**. I write code to turn hard concepts into experiences students can explore, test and get wrong safely.

My principle: **scientific rigor first, teaching in its service** — my simulators aim for the highest physiological fidelity possible, never trading accuracy for oversimplification.

On the side (and, I hope, the future), I design deduction and investigation games at **Dragon Games**.
